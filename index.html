AboutHi, I’m Maryam

I’m a digital designer. Most of my days are spent in VHDL, a simulator, and a lab bench next to an FPGA board.

I am a PhD candidate in Electronics, Electrical Engineering, Automation and Signal Processing (EEATS) at the LCIS Laboratory, Grenoble INP-Esisar, Université Grenoble Alpes. I am supervised by Dr. Romain Siragusa and co-supervised by Prof. David Hély, Dr. Louis Morge-Rollet and Dr. Ioana Vatajelu. My thesis is Development of a Wireless RF Link on FPGA and Associated Security Tools.

My core expertise is digital design: RTL in VHDL, simulation and verification with ModelSim, FPGA implementation with Vivado and Quartus, and synthesis with Design Compiler. I apply it to hardware security, from PUF authentication circuits to countermeasures against physical fault attacks.

Before the PhD, I completed a Master in Embedded Systems at Grenoble INP-Esisar and a Master in Computer Architecture Engineering at Iran University of Science and Technology, where I graduated first in my program.

ResearchWhat I work on

Digital design for RF on FPGA

Digital architectures that run a wireless RF link directly in FPGA fabric, including near-field antennas implemented on Artix-7 devices.

RFFPGAAntennas

Hardware authentication

Ring-oscillator and PUF-based authentication that gives IoT devices a hardware fingerprint they cannot easily clone.
